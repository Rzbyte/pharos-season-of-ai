Pharos Inspector

Paste a contract address or a transaction hash and it is read through Pharos Chain MCP, separately from the snapshot above.

A contract: its ERC-20 name, symbol, decimals and total supply, at one block. A transaction: its status, block, sender, recipient, value, gas and number of logs.

Accepts a contract address (42 characters) or a transaction hash (66).
