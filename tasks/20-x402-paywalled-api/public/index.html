      How it runs

      1. 1eth_callThe asset, read from the chain: its EIP-712 name and version (what an authorization signs, never assumed) and whether it answers EIP-3009, which the exact scheme settles by
      2. 2402GET /data without a payment: x402's resource server answers 402 Payment Required, its terms in PAYMENT-REQUIRED: so much USDC, to whom, on eip155:1672, by the exact scheme
      3. 3signx402's client signs an EIP-3009 TransferWithAuthorization for exactly those terms, only in this asset and never above its cap, and asks again with it in PAYMENT-SIGNATURE
      4. 4verifyx402's facilitator checks the signature, then simulates transferWithAuthorization on chain (eth_call through Chain MCP); refused, it reads why: the payer's balance, the token's name and version, the nonce
      5. 5settleVerified, the data is made, then the payment settled: transferWithAuthorization sent through Chain MCP (send_raw_transaction) and its receipt checked for the Transfer. Only then is the data released, with PAYMENT-RESPONSE

      Run it yourself
      
        
      cd agent && npm ci
node dist/src/cli.js x402 --proof x402-mainnet.json
node dist/src/cli.js verify x402-mainnet.json
# on Atlantic, paid and settled, with your testnet keys:
PRIVATE_KEY=0x... FACILITATOR_PRIVATE_KEY=0x... node dist/src/cli.js x402 --network atlantic-testnet --execute --proof x402-atlantic.json
# or as a real API, and a client paying it:
FACILITATOR_PRIVATE_KEY=0x... node dist/src/cli.js x402 serve --network atlantic-testnet --execute
PRIVATE_KEY=0x... node dist/src/cli.js x402 pay http://127.0.0.1:4020/data --network atlantic-testnet
      
        
      A payment cannot be replayed whole (a fresh nonce, the time it ran, a key made for it), so the verifier checks its facts against the recorded Chain MCP answers: the asset read again, the 402's terms exactly what was asked, the authorization signed by the payer for exactly those terms within the cap, the facilitator's verdict as the chain answered its simulation, and, when settled, the one transaction sent: transferWithAuthorization of exactly that authorization, signed by the facilitator, its receipt holding the Transfer.
