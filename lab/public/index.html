About this lab
What is this?
One builder's entries for the Pharos Season of AI Agent Arena: each task is a small tool that does one job against Pharos, with its source and a committed proof of a real run.
What does each task use?
01 and 02 read Pharos through Pharos Chain MCP. 03 records onboarding with the Pharos Developer Docs MCP. 04 queries the official docs over their public HTTPS interface, not MCP.
Where is the proof?
On each task's page, under Proof, and in its folder of the repository, opens in a new tab.
